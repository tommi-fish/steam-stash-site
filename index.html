Steam / Stash

A free, open-source desktop app that scrapes your Steam trade, market, and inventory history — and stores everything locally in SQLite. No servers. No telemetry. Just your data.

Download Source
· Features ·

Local Database

Everything stored in SQLite via better-sqlite3 and drizzle-orm. No cloud, no servers, no telemetry — your trade history stays on your machine.

Steam Auth

Browserless login via steam-session with refresh token persistence. Manual cookie paste fallback. No password stored.

Full History Sync

Scrapes your complete Steam trade, market, and case opening history. Cursor-based pagination, incremental sync, resume support, 429 backoff.

JSON & CSV Export

Export your trade history, market transactions, and case openings to JSON or CSV with a single click. Full data ownership.

Screenshot Composer

Generate shareable screenshots of trades, market transactions, and case openings. Multiple themes, toggleable elements, multi-select compositing.

Dark Monochrome UI

Oklch-based theme system with 11 accent colours, density controls, and a meticulous Nothing-style dark aesthetic. No Tailwind — all inline.
